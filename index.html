/ Daily Routine Guide

Daily Morning & Night Skincare Guide

Morning Glow & Sun Protection Routine (4 Steps)

Step 1 — Cleanse: Wash your face with a gentle pH-balanced Face Wash to remove overnight oil.

Step 2 — Tone: Pat a hydrating Toner to balance skin pH and prep for active absorption.

Step 3 — Treat: Apply 3–4 drops of Vitamin C Serum to brighten dullness.

Step 4 — Protect: Finish with 2 fingers' worth of SPF 50+ PA+++ Sunscreen 15 minutes before sun exposure.

Night Repair & Hydration Routine (3 Steps)

Step 1 — Deep Cleanse: Remove sunscreen, dust, and excess sebum thoroughly before bed.

Step 2 — Repair: Use Niacinamide 10% (for oily/acne skin) or Alpha Arbutin 2% (for dark spots).

Step 3 — Lock Moisture: Seal everything with a cooling Barrier Repair Gel Moisturizer so skin heals overnight.
